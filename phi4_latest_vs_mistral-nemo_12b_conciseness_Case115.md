1. **VRAM/Compute Constraints:**
   - **VRAM Requirement (V):** \( V = \frac{D \times A}{C} \)
     - \( D \): Data size
     - \( A \): Algorithm complexity
     - \( C \): Cache efficiency

2. **Operational Perimeter:**
   - **Data Sovereignty (S):** \( S = \frac{L \times P}{B} \)
     - \( L \): Local storage capacity
     - \( P \): Processing power
     - \( B \): Bandwidth availability

3. **Intellectual Autarky (I):**
   - **Autarky Index (I):** \( I = \frac{R \times F}{E} \)
     - \( R \): Redundancy in data storage
     - \( F \): Flexibility of algorithm adaptation
     - \( E \): External dependency
