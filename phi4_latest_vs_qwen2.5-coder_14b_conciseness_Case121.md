3. **Network Independence**: To achieve data sovereignty, local networks must be capable of operating without external inputs. This requires robust local storage solutions and the ability to process data offline. 4. **Hardware Parameters**: Establishing intellectual autarky requires hardware capable of supporting long-term operations without external maintenance. This includes energy-efficient processors, sufficient RAM for data caching, and durable storage solutions. The operational perimeter of a self-sustaining offline data fortress is defined by the hardware's ability to maintain performance over time, factoring in wear and tear, energy consumption, and environmental conditions.
